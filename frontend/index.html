    Frequently Asked Questions

    Essential cyber safety rules every digital citizen must know.

    How does ScamShield AI differentiate between real and scam alerts? ▼
    Legitimate organizations (banks, courier services, government agencies) never threaten immediate account closure within hours or request one-time passwords (OTPs) and UPI PINs over SMS. ScamShield cross-examines tone, urgency, URL anomalies, and psychological coercion.
    Can I paste screenshots from WhatsApp or Telegram? ▼
    Yes! You can either upload an image file (PNG/JPG) or copy a screenshot to your clipboard and simply press Ctrl+V anywhere on this page to automatically load it into the Screenshot Scanner.
    What should I do if a message returns a HIGH RISK score? ▼
    Do not click any embedded links, do not reply, and do not call numbers provided inside the message. Verify independently through the official bank app or website. If financial data was shared, immediately contact your bank to freeze debit/credit cards.
